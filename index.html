Why Study MBBS Abroad?

Affordable Fees

Low tuition fees compared to private medical colleges in your home country.

Global Recognition

Degrees recognized by WHO, MCI, and other international medical councils.

Quality Education

World-class infrastructure and experienced faculty.

No Entrance Exams

Many universities abroad do not require entrance exams like NEET, making admission easier.

Multicultural Exposure

Study in an international environment with students from various backgrounds.

Advanced Medical Facilities

Gain hands-on experience in hospitals equipped with modern medical technology.

English-Medium Programs

Most universities offer courses in English, eliminating language barriers.

Internship Opportunities

Students can gain real-world experience through internships in top hospitals.

Better Student-Teacher Ratio

Smaller class sizes ensure better interaction between students and professors.

Admission Process & Eligibility

Eligibility Criteria

  • Minimum 50% in 12th grade (PCB).
  • NEET qualification (for Indian students).
  • Valid passport.

Admission Process

  • Submit application form.
  • Receive offer letter.
  • Pay tuition fees.
  • Apply for student visa.
